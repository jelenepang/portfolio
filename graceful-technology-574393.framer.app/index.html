what i like to think

As a dedicated student in Simon Fraser University’s Interactive Arts and Technology program, I excel in creative thinking and iterative design. My passion encompasses diverse areas of visual design, with a focus on creating engaging, user-friendly digital interfaces.

what i think i like

Beyond my professional interests, I am enthusiastic about a variety of personal pursuits. Having previously played badminton competitively, it continues to serve as a source of stress relief and recreation with friends. More recently, I’ve developed a mild obsession with 8-ball pool.

At other times, you’ll likely find me retreated to a cozy corner for some creative downtime, immersed in a crocheting or knitting project or many other kinds of crafting.
